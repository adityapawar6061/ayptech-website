Ownership

Use ours, bring your own, or both

AYP Tech is a finished product, not a lock-in. If you already have carrier relationships or committed model spend, keep them — the platform is built to sit on top of what you have.

BYOC

Bring your own carrier

Already running Exotel, Bonvoice, Plivo, Twilio or Telnyx? Connect your existing SIP trunk and keep the numbers you have published and the rates you negotiated. No porting, no migration window, no customer-facing change — and no AYP Tech markup on your minutes.

  • Your carrier stays carrier of record
  • Existing DLT registrations still apply
  • Zero migration
Self-service keys

Bring your own model keys

Enterprise buyers can supply their own OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS Bedrock, Groq, Mistral, ElevenLabs or Deepgram credentials. Inference bills against your existing committed contract, and AYP Tech charges the platform fee alone.

  • Model spend sits on your own contract
  • Your cloned voices and fine-tunes come with you
  • Platform fee only
Hybrid

Mix the two, per agent

Nothing here is workspace-wide. Run AYP Tech-managed models for most agents and self-service keys for the specialised ones, or a managed Indian number for outbound alongside your own trunk for inbound. Every choice is made per agent.

  • Managed and BYO side by side
  • Different carriers on different agents
  • Switch either way, any time
