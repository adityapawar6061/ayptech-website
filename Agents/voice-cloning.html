FAQ

Voice cloning questions

How does voice cloning on AYP Tech work?

Record about 30 seconds of speech, and Cartesia builds a voice model that captures your tone, cadence and warmth. You then attach that cloned voice to any agent or campaign, and it starts making real calls in that voice.

Whose voice can I clone?

Your own voice, your best salesperson's voice, or your founder's voice for VIP customers. You can deploy different cloned voices by use case, by agent or by campaign.

Is voice cloning actually live, or a roadmap feature?

It is live in production. More than 35 AYP Tech agents already run on Cartesia voice cloning today, from whitelabel sales to real estate outbound.

Will callers be able to tell it is AI?

A cloned Cartesia voice sounds warm and natural rather than robotic, so most callers experience it as a real person. The agent still handles turn-taking, interruptions and mid-call actions like any AYP Tech agent.

Does voice cloning work for Indian languages?

Yes. Cloned voices work alongside AYP Tech's 70+ languages, so an agent can speak in Hindi, Hinglish or a regional language while still sounding like the person whose voice was cloned.
