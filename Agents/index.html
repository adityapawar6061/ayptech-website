What is an AI voice agent?

An AI voice agent is an autonomous system that can listen, speak, and hold natural phone conversations like a human. It combines speech recognition, large language models, and text-to-speech so it can understand callers, respond intelligently, keep context across the call, and take real actions. Unlike a legacy IVR menu, a AYP Tech agent doesn't rely on rigid "press 1 for sales" scripts. It understands intent in plain language and can book appointments, look up an order, update your CRM or warm-transfer to a human, all without a person on the line.

How do AYP Tech voice agents work?

You describe what the agent should handle in plain language, connect the tools it needs, such as your CRM, calendar or knowledge base, and AYP Tech wires up the speech recognition, reasoning and voice pipeline behind the scenes. The agent listens on a live call, decides what to say or do next, and can call out to your systems in real time before responding.

Do I need to write code to build an agent?

No. AYP Tech's no-code platform lets you design, test, and deploy a voice agent from the dashboard. If you want deeper control, the AYP Tech API, MCP server, and SIP trunking let developers integrate agents directly into existing systems and carriers.

Which languages and phone providers are supported?

AYP Tech agents support 70+ languages out of the box. You can use a AYP Tech-provided number or bring your own SIP carrier such as Plivo, Twilio or Telnyx for inbound and outbound calling.

Can agents integrate with my CRM and other tools?

Yes. Agents can call out to your CRM, helpdesk, calendar, or any REST API mid-call to look up records, update fields, or trigger workflows. AI tools like Claude can also manage your AYP Tech account directly through MCP.

Is my call data secure?

All calls and API access are scoped to your account with API-key or OAuth 2.1 authentication. Call recordings, transcripts, and customer data are encrypted in transit and at rest.
