Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our per-minute billing and self-hosted lifetime ownership.

How does the Self-Hosted One-Time Setup work? What are my ongoing costs?

With our Self-Hosted Enterprise option, our senior engineering team sets up, fine-tunes, and installs the entire Voice AI platform and Niwan Office Suite directly into your company's private on-premise servers or cloud VPC (AWS, Azure, GCP). You receive complete ownership with full source containers, deployment playbooks, and zero vendor dependency. Your recurring platform cost to AYP Tech is ₹0 forever. You only pay your chosen telecom provider (like Airtel, Tata Tele, Jio, or Twilio) directly for raw SIP minutes with zero middleman markup.

How does the ₹3 vs. ₹4 per minute managed cloud pricing work?

Both managed plans are billed precisely per-second with zero rounding. On the ₹3/min Growth plan, you get our complete calling platform, stereo recordings, SIP connectivity, and database persistence with CSV exports. On the ₹4/min Enterprise Pro plan, you get everything plus ultra-low latency (<400ms), omnichannel WhatsApp/SMS dispatch, direct CRM sync, priority 24/7 support, and complete access to the Niwan AI Office Suite.

Why choose Self-Hosted On-Premise over cloud SaaS?

Self-hosting gives you two massive advantages: Cost Savings and Absolute Data Sovereignty. If you run 200,000+ minutes a month, paying SaaS per-minute fees adds up quickly; with on-premise, your platform fee is ₹0 forever. Furthermore, for banks, NBFCs, healthcare providers, and enterprises bound by strict regulatory standards (RBI, DPDP Act, HIPAA), your customer voice recordings and financial data never leave your own firewall.

What is the Niwan AI Office Suite included in Pro and Self-Hosted?

Niwan Office is an enterprise-grade AI executive productivity suite. It turns your customer calls into executive summaries, automatically drafts contracts and customer proposals, creates follow-up action items, and automates repetitive office workflows across your sales and operations teams.

How do I access and download my call data and recordings?

All caller interactions, audio recordings, sentiment tags, and transcriptions are saved automatically in your encrypted database. You can filter, search, and download your entire dataset in clean CSV or Excel format with a single click from your dashboard at any time.

Can I bring my own SIP trunk or Indian mobile numbers?

Yes! You can bring your own carrier (BYOC) through SIP trunking (Tata Tele, Airtel, Jio, Exotel, Twilio, Plivo) or purchase managed Indian 98 and 94 series mobile virtual numbers directly through AYP Tech.

Why is per-second billing important?

Most telephony providers round up to the nearest minute. If a call lasts 12 seconds because a customer quickly hung up, you are billed for 60 seconds! With AYP Tech's per-second billing, a 12-second call costs you just ₹0.60, cutting unnecessary telephony waste by up to 40%.
