Pricing FAQ

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The ₹599 Pay As You Go plan gives you 7 days of full access with 500 credits, so you can build and test a real voice agent before committing to a monthly plan.

How does AYP Tech pricing work?

After the trial, you can stay on the metered Developer plan at ₹15,000 a month or move to an Unlimited plan. Unlimited calling starts at ₹19,999 a month for one agent, ₹39,999 for three agents, and ₹79,999 for ten agents with white-label access included. Enterprise pricing is custom.

What does "unlimited" actually include?

Unlimited plans cover calling to Indian numbers within TRAI-permitted hours, under a fair-use policy with an eight-hour daily calling window. Concurrency is capped by plan at 2, 6, or 15 calls. International calls are billed separately per second.

Why per-second billing instead of per minute?

Most platforms bill per minute, so a 5-second unanswered call can be charged as a full minute. In real outbound campaigns, 30 to 40% of calls may go unanswered, which can significantly increase costs. AYP Tech bills per second, and on Unlimited plans you don't count minutes at all.

Can I bring my own telephony carrier?

Yes. You can bring your own carrier, such as Plivo, Twilio, or Telnyx, and route calls over your own SIP trunk, or use AYP Tech's managed 98 and 94 series mobile numbers.

Do you offer white-label or reseller pricing?

Yes. White-label access is included on the Unlimited Business plan, with margins up to 50% plus sales training and deal-closing support. Visit the Whitelabel Programme page to apply.
