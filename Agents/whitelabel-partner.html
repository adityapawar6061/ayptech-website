FAQ

White-label questions

Margins, training, branding and what it actually takes to launch your agency.

How much margin do I keep as a AYP Tech white-label partner?

Up to 50%. You set your own client pricing and there is no revenue share on top — your cost is the flat ₹79,999/month Unlimited Business plan that includes white-label. Because that number doesn't move as you add clients, your effective margin rises with every client you sign.

What training and certification do partners get?

Expert sales training on how to pitch voice AI to Indian businesses, prompt engineering sessions on building agents that actually convert, and a voice AI certification for you and your team. Training is delivered live by the AYP Tech team, not as recorded courses.

Will AYP Tech help me close my own clients?

Yes. We join your client demos, answer technical questions during negotiation, and stay reachable in a dedicated Slack channel for your team. You stay the brand on the call — we're there as your solutions engineer.

Do my clients ever see the AYP Tech brand?

No. The dashboard, agent builder, analytics, documentation and domain all carry your brand. Your clients sign with you, pay you, and are supported by you — AYP Tech stays behind the platform.

Do I need developers to run a AYP Tech white-label agency?

No. The platform is no-code end to end — agent building, telephony, campaigns and reporting are all visual. Solo operators run agencies on AYP Tech without writing a line of code, though an API and MCP server are there if you want to automate deeper.

How do I apply to the white-label programme?

Submit the application form on this page. The partnerships team reviews every application and responds within two business days with a 15-minute discovery call to work out your first verticals and pricing. The programme requires a minimum of 50,000 voice minutes per month.

Which voice models and telephony do I get to resell?

All of them, under one platform fee — AWS Nova 2 Sonic, OpenAI GPT Realtime, Azure Voice Live, Cartesia voice cloning, ElevenLabs and xAI Grok, plus Indian mobile-series numbers and bring-your-own-carrier support for Exotel, Plivo, Twilio and Telnyx.
