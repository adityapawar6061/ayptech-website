Core Tenets

The four laws of our engineering.

1. Determinism Over Fluff

We never deploy AI for the sake of buzzwords. If a simple webhook or database trigger does the job better and cheaper than an LLM prompt, we build the deterministic trigger.

2. Absolute Data Sovereignty

Your proprietary business numbers and customer leads never train third-party public models. We enforce zero-retention enterprise API endpoints and strict token-level isolation.

3. Extreme Time-to-Value

No 6-month enterprise scoping marathons. Our average project goes from initial workflow audit to production deployment in 14 days or less.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

AI agents handle the cognitive grunt work, but critical high-stakes triggers always include human approval buttons on Slack or WhatsApp.
