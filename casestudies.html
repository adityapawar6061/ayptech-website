LOGISTICS & FREIGHT FORWARDING · NAVI MUMBAI

Recovering ₹3.2L/Month by Automating 2,400+ Waybills & Invoices

₹3.2L Recovered / Month

The Bottleneck

Truck drivers photographed physical delivery proofs and sent them over WhatsApp. Two full-time accountants spent 7 hours a day manually matching receipts to vendor bills, causing frequent missed deductions.

The AI Automation System

We deployed a WhatsApp bot connected to a multimodal OCR pipeline. Drivers snap photos $\rightarrow$ Vision AI extracts consignment numbers & stamps $\rightarrow$ auto-matches against database $\rightarrow$ reconciles in Tally.

Measurable Impact

100% elimination of billing leakage. Processing time plummeted from 4 days to 3 minutes per consignment. The client recovered ₹38.4 Lakhs in the first year alone.
